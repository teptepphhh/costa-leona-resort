# House rules (fictional)

- Quiet hours: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM. No loud music or karaoke during quiet hours.
- Visitors: day visitors must register at the front desk and pay a day-visitor fee of PHP 500 per person. Visitors must leave by 8:00 PM and may not stay overnight in rooms.
- Pets: not allowed, except registered service animals.
- Smoking: only in the marked smoking area near the beach bar. Not in rooms, villas or restaurants.
- Alcohol: guests under 18 may not drink alcohol.
- Drones: not allowed without written permission from the front desk.
- Glass items are not allowed at the beach or the pool.
- Fires, fireworks and open-flame cooking in rooms are not allowed.
- Damage: guests are responsible for damage to resort property. Lost key card replacement costs PHP 300.
- Valuables: use the in-room safe. The resort is not responsible for items left unattended on the beach.
- Safety: in an emergency, follow the evacuation signs in your room and gather at the lobby.
- The resort may ask guests who break the rules repeatedly to leave without refund.
