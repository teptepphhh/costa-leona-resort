# Getting to Costa Leona Resort and common questions (fictional)

## How to get there
- By car: about 3.5 hours from Metro Manila (invented route): take the expressway to the Punta Leona exit, then follow the coastal road for 18 km. Look for the white lighthouse sign.
- Resort shuttle from the city pick-up point (Mall of Asia side, invented): departs Friday and Saturday at 7:00 AM. Return trips depart the resort on Sunday and Monday at 1:00 PM. Fare PHP 700 per person each way. Book at least 2 days ahead through the front desk.
- By bus: take a provincial bus bound for "Punta Leona", get off at the town plaza, then take a tricycle (about PHP 150, 15 minutes) to the resort.
- Nearest airport: none in the area. Guests usually come by road.
- GPS note: the map pin may show the old fishing pier. Use the resort name "Costa Leona Resort" and call the front desk if lost.

## Frequently asked questions
- Is there an ATM? No. The nearest ATM is in the town plaza, 15 minutes away. Bring cash for small purchases.
- Is there mobile signal? Yes, but it can be weak at the far end of the beach. Wi-Fi is available in rooms and lobby.
- Can you arrange an airport or bus pick-up? Only the scheduled shuttle above. Private transfers are not offered.
- Is the resort suitable for wheelchair users? The lobby, restaurant and Garden Rooms on the ground floor have ramps. The beach path is sandy and not wheelchair-friendly. Please call the front desk before booking.
- Do you offer weddings or events? Not through Leona. Please email the front desk.
- What is the weather like? Dry season is November to May. Typhoon season can close the beach; the front desk will contact guests.
- Does the resort give medical care? No. The front desk can call the nearest clinic (20 minutes away). For emergencies, call +63 900 000 0911 (fictional).
- What can Leona do? Leona answers questions about rates, times, rules and directions. Leona cannot make bookings, change reservations, take payments, or give medical advice.
