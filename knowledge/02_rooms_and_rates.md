# Room types and nightly rates (fictional, PHP)

| Room type | Beds | Max guests | Size | Rate per night |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Garden Room | 1 queen bed | 2 adults | 24 sqm | PHP 4,200 |
| Seaview Room | 1 king bed or 2 single beds | 2 adults + 1 child | 30 sqm | PHP 5,800 |
| Lioness Family Villa | 1 king bed + 2 single beds | 4 adults + 1 child | 55 sqm | PHP 9,500 |
| Sunset Pool Villa | 1 king bed, private plunge pool | 2 adults | 48 sqm | PHP 12,900 |

- Extra bed: PHP 900 per night (Seaview Room and Lioness Family Villa only, subject to availability).
- Children aged 5 and under stay free when using existing beds. Ages 6 to 11 count as 0.5 guest for breakfast only.
- Breakfast for two is included in every rate. Breakfast is served 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM at Sol y Mar restaurant.
- All rooms have air conditioning, a fan, a minibar, a safe, a kettle and free Wi-Fi.
- Only the Seaview Room, Lioness Family Villa and Sunset Pool Villa face the sea. The Garden Room faces the garden.
- Smoking is not allowed in any room. A cleaning fee of PHP 5,000 applies if smoking is detected.
- Bookings are made by emailing the front desk or calling. Leona (this assistant) cannot take or change bookings.
