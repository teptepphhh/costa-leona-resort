# Amenities and dining (fictional)

## Facilities and opening hours
- Main pool: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Children must be accompanied by an adult. No lifeguard after 6:00 PM.
- Beach: open all day. Swimming is allowed in the roped area from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM only.
- Kayak hut: 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Free kayak use for 1 hour per room per day, extra hours PHP 300 per kayak. Life vests are provided and required.
- Spa "Alon": 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, by appointment. A 60-minute massage costs PHP 1,800.
- Gym corner: 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM, free for guests.
- Free Wi-Fi in rooms and in the lobby. Speed is suitable for email and video calls but not for large uploads.
- Free parking for guests, up to one car per room.

## Dining
- Sol y Mar restaurant: breakfast 6:30 AM to 10:00 AM, lunch 11:30 AM to 2:30 PM, dinner 6:00 PM to 9:30 PM. Filipino and seafood dishes.
- Marea beach bar: 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, drinks and light snacks.
- Room service: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, with a PHP 150 service fee.
- Vegetarian dishes are available. Guests with food allergies must tell the restaurant staff in person.
- Outside food and drinks are allowed in rooms, but not at the pool.
- Sample prices: grilled fish of the day PHP 650, chicken adobo PHP 420, halo-halo PHP 220, bottled water PHP 60.
