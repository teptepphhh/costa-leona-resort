# Check-in, check-out and booking policies (fictional)

- Standard check-in time: 2:00 PM.
- Standard check-out time: 11:00 AM.
- Early check-in: from 10:00 AM for PHP 1,000, only if the room is ready. Before 10:00 AM is not possible.
- Late check-out: until 2:00 PM for PHP 1,200. After 2:00 PM, a full extra night is charged. Late check-out must be requested the day before.
- Luggage storage: free of charge before check-in and after check-out, at the front desk until 6:00 PM.
- Valid ID is shown at check-in. The resort does not need guests to send ID or card details by chat.
- Deposit: one night's rate is paid at booking and is deducted from the final bill.
- Cancellation: free up to 7 days before arrival. Cancelling 3 to 6 days before arrival costs one night. Cancelling 2 days or less before arrival, or not showing up, costs the full stay.
- Peak dates (December 20 to January 5, Holy Week, and long weekends) need a 3-night minimum stay and have no free cancellation.
- Changing dates: free once, if requested at least 7 days before arrival and subject to availability.
- Payment methods at the resort: cash (PHP), major credit cards and bank transfer. Personal cheques are not accepted.
